# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good faith while checking evidence, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment or discrimination, threats, doxxing, credential or private-data disclosure, and sharing copyrighted ROMs, extracted assets, or proprietary toolchain files.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and the legal or technical integrity of the project.
